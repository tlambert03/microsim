*[PSF]: Point spread function: the image of a single point emitter
*[AU]: Airy unit: the diameter of the first dark ring of the Airy disk, 1.22 λ / NA
*[NA]: Numerical aperture of the objective lens, n·sin(θ)
*[QE]: Quantum efficiency: the probability that a photon creates a photoelectron
*[QY]: Quantum yield: the fraction of absorbed photons that are re-emitted as fluorescence
*[ADU]: Analog-to-digital unit, also called a gray value
*[ADC]: Analog-to-digital converter
*[CCD]: Charge-coupled device camera
*[EMCCD]: Electron-multiplying charge-coupled device camera
*[sCMOS]: Scientific complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor camera
*[CMOS]: Complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor camera
*[PMT]: Photomultiplier tube
*[HyD]: Hybrid photodetector (photocathode + avalanche diode), e.g. Leica HyD
*[GaAsP]: Gallium arsenide phosphide, a high-QE photocathode material
*[HV]: High voltage, which sets the gain of a PMT
*[CIC]: Clock-induced charge
*[ENF]: Excess noise factor
*[FPbase]: The fluorescent protein database, fpbase.org
*[COSEM]: Cell Organelle Segmentation in Electron Microscopy (Janelia)
*[FIB-SEM]: Focused ion beam scanning electron microscopy
